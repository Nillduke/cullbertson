CONVERSATIONS WORKSPACE

Local route: /admin/inbox. Direct lead URLs /admin/inbox/{kind}/{id} open the
same workspace with that lead selected. Original full-detail view remains at
/admin/inbox/{kind}/{id}/details. Existing lead status controls still work.

All quote requests (including partials), contact enquiries, feedback and imported
WordPress submissions are queried directly. Original payloads remain unchanged.
There is one conversation per submission, identified by kind + source_id.
No automatic merging of different customers or submissions takes place.

Features: combined conversation list, search and original source/date/type/status/
service/completion filters; All, Unread, Recent (7 days), Starred; owner assignment
to CMS admins; shared team read/unread markers; threaded internal notes and reply
drafts; editable saved reply drafts; private attachments and full submission fields.
Notes and draft messages carry an author and timestamp. Duplicate submissions of
the same message token do not create duplicate messages. Draft edits are scoped
to the selected conversation. All content is escaped on output.

OUTGOING CUSTOMER EMAIL
The composer uses the existing Admin > Email & SMTP settings. Save reply draft
never sends. Send email explicitly queues and attempts delivery to the lead email,
not to the notification recipients. Internal notes are never sent. Existing drafts
are not sent automatically. Edit draft loads an unsent draft into the composer;
Send email sends it. Queued/accepted messages cannot be edited.

Mail deliveries use a unique message key, atomic claim and stable Message-ID.
The thread shows queued, sending, accepted by SMTP or failed; SMTP acceptance is
not proof of inbox delivery. Failures have sanitized errors and the existing
mail:deliver retry schedule. Admin > Email & SMTP > Delivery history also supports
manual retry. As with SMTP generally, a process failure after remote acceptance
can leave delivery uncertain; retries are not a guarantee of exactly-once delivery.

Customer messages use the configured From and Reply-To. Incoming email replies,
mailbox sync and SMS are not implemented. No mailbox credentials beyond existing
outgoing SMTP settings are requested by this change.

Thread refresh polls the protected JSON endpoint every 20 seconds while visible.
The composer is not replaced by polling. The inbox list can be refreshed separately.
Reading a lead changes its shared conversation read marker, not its lead status.
Mark unread returns to the unread inbox. New source updates make the thread unread.

Database migration: 2026_11_20_180000_create_inbox_conversations.
Tables: inbox_conversations, inbox_messages. Both are private and are scrubbed from
the cPanel content-snapshot package. Existing lead tables are not altered.
Only auth + EnsureCmsAdmin routes expose messages, details or management actions.

Responsive UI: three columns on wide screens, contact-details drawer on narrower
desktops/tablets, list/thread navigation and details drawer on mobile.
