Online forms and saved quote requests

Quote forms save the details submitted when you select Continue, including unfinished requests. Contact permission and the optional request for text messages are recorded separately. General forms should not be used to send passwords, payment card details, Social Security numbers or sensitive medical information.

Privacy requests and contact preferences

Use Privacy Choices to ask about access, corrections, deletion, sharing or contact preferences. Requests are reviewed by our agency team and may require identity checks. Applicable law, insurance administration and record-retention obligations can affect the response. Our policy above states that we do not sell or rent personal information.

Cookies and other technology

The application uses session cookies and quote-flow identifiers in browser storage to support security and form progress. Clearing browser storage does not delete records saved on our server. Read Cookies and Other Technology for details and browser choices.
